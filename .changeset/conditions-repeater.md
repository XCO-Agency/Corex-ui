New `ConditionsRepeater`: repeated condition rows (rounded cards with field and operator pills, a value area under a divider, a trash button that shows on hover or keyboard focus and removes the row at once), an add button with an optional menu, an end slot, an empty state, and a `maxItems` limit with a note. Ships with `ConditionsRepeater.Pill`, `ConditionsRepeater.Options`, and, for multi-value rows, `ConditionsRepeater.TagsInput` (chips joined by AND / OR, added on Enter or a comma) and `ConditionsRepeater.MatchMode` (the All / Any pill, lockable).
